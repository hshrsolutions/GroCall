# GroCall — salesperson guide

Status: Phase 0. The phone app and call screens arrive in later phases; this guide grows with them.

1. Sign in and pick **GroCall** on the apps screen (`https://<your site>/apps`), or open **`https://<your site>/calls`** directly.
2. **Today** will show your calls, talk time, missed calls you still need to return, and follow-ups due.
3. *(Phase 3)* Install the GroCall app on your phone, scan the pairing QR code from your admin, and pick
   your **company SIM**. Only calls on the company SIM are tracked; your personal SIM is never read.
4. *(Phase 3)* Turn on your phone's built-in **automatic call recording**; the app shows how for your brand.
5. *(Phase 4)* After each call, choose what happened (interested, call back, not reachable…), add a note and
   the next follow-up date.
